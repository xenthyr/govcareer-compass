# 2. Examination Identity

The WBCS examination is a recruitment examination through which the Commission recruits to specified West Bengal services and posts according to the applicable examination notification and service rules.

The examination must therefore be represented as:

    Exam
      ↓
    Advertisement / Cycle
      ↓
    Services / Posts
      ↓
    Departments / Organisations

# 4. Important 2024 Scheme Rule

The official Commission announced that:

- WBCS Examination 2024 would continue under the old scheme and syllabus;
- the older scheme arose under Notification No. 437-PAR(WBCS)/1D-389/13 dated 27 February 2014;
- the revised scheme and syllabus would come into effect from WBCS Examination 2025.

This distinction is mandatory.

Do not display the 2025 revised WBCS pattern as the scheme for the 2024 examination.

# 8. WBCS Service/Post Research

For every advertised service or post record:

- service name;
- post name;
- group;
- department;
- organisation;
- cadre;
- pay level;
- minimum qualification;
- language;
- physical requirement;
- medical requirement;
- recruitment method;
- age;
- reservation;
- domicile;
- career route.

Do not assume all WBCS services have identical practical duties.
