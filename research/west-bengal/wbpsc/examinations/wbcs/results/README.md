# WBCS — Results, Marks and Recommendation Archive

## Purpose

This directory preserves official WBCS results and recommendation information.

---

# 1. Result Lifecycle

Keep separate records for:

- Preliminary result;
- Main result;
- Personality Test qualification;
- final merit/result;
- marks;
- recommended candidates;
- appointment recommendation;
- revised result;
- additional recommendation;
- non-joining vacancy recommendation;
- other official result-related notices.

---

# 2. Important Rule

A:

    result

is not the same as:

    final recommendation

and:

    final recommendation

is not the same as:

    appointment

These must remain separate.

---

# 3. WBCS 2024

As of the research baseline:

**31 August 2026**

the WBCS 2024 cycle has progressed through the Preliminary Examination and final answer-key stage.

The research workspace must continue checking the Commission for:

- Preliminary result;
- list of candidates qualified for Main;
- Main examination schedule;
- Main result;
- Personality Test schedule;
- final recommendation;
- later appointment-related notices.

---

# 4. Historical Results

Historical WBCS results must be preserved for:

- understanding recruitment lifecycle;
- promotion/career research;
- competition analysis where official statistics exist;
- historical cutoff research where officially published.

---

# 5. Result Storage

Recommended filenames:

    wbcs-2024-prelim-result-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf
    wbcs-2024-main-result-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf
    wbcs-2024-final-recommendation-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf

Replace `YYYY-MM-DD` with the actual official publication date.

Do not invent dates.

---

# 6. Marks Data

If the Commission publishes marks:

Store separately from the result document.

Record:

- stage;
- candidate population;
- marks type;
- publication date;
- source ID.

Do not infer marks from unofficial coaching sites.

---

# 7. Revised Results

If a result is revised:

    Original result
          ↓
    Revised result
          ↓
    Current effective result

The revised document must not overwrite the original.

---

# 8. Production Rule

The website may display current official result status.

Historical result documents may remain accessible from the research/source interface.

Do not generate a current selection-status claim without current official evidence.
