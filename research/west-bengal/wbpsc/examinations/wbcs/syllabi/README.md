# WBCS — Syllabus and Scheme Archive

## Purpose

This directory preserves official WBCS scheme and syllabus documents by examination cycle.

---

# 1. Critical Version Rule

WBCS syllabus information is time-sensitive.

Never treat one syllabus as universally applicable to all WBCS examinations.

Each examination cycle must have an explicit scheme version.

---

# 2. WBCS 2024

The official Commission announced that:

**WBCS Examination 2024 would be conducted under the old scheme and syllabus.**

The Commission also stated that the new/revised scheme and syllabus would apply from:

**WBCS Examination 2025**

# 7. Syllabus Extraction

For each cycle record:

- preliminary subjects;
- main papers;
- optional subjects;
- compulsory papers;
- marks;
- duration;
- qualifying papers;
- language papers;
- personality test;
- applicable group;
- negative marking where formally stated;
- standard/level;
- changes from previous cycle.

Do not copy excessive copyrighted syllabus text into the research notes.

Use concise factual summaries and preserve the official document.

---

# 8. Important 2024 Fact

Historical official WBCS syllabus material for the 2023 cycle describes the old preliminary pattern as one General Studies paper containing 200 objective questions for 200 marks over 2½ hours, with areas including English Composition, General Science, Current Affairs, History of India, Geography of India with special reference to West Bengal, Indian Polity and Economy, Indian National Movement and General Mental Ability.

Because WBCS 2024 was explicitly retained under the old scheme, this historical scheme is relevant to the 2024 research cycle.

Source:

Source classification:
