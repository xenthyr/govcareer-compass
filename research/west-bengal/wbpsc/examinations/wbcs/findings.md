# 8. Candidate Eligibility — B.A. English Honours

The candidate's:

    B.A. English Honours

may satisfy the general degree requirement for WBCS where the advertisement requires a recognised university degree.

However, WBCS eligibility cannot be reduced to the degree alone.

The recommendation engine must check:

- age;
- language requirements;
- reservation;
- domicile or other conditions where applicable;
- physical requirements for applicable services;
- any post/service-specific condition.

---

# 9. Bengali Requirement

Where the applicable WBCS recruitment rules require ability to:

- read;
- write;
- speak Bengali,

this must be represented as a separate hard-eligibility condition.

The applicable rule may contain a specific exception for candidates whose mother tongue is Nepali.

The production eligibility rule must point to the actual applicable official advertisement/rule.

Do not treat:

    English Honours

as evidence that the candidate satisfies a Bengali-language requirement.

The candidate must answer the language question separately.

---

# 10. West Bengal Police Service

WBCS includes recruitment to the West Bengal Police Service.

Police-service eligibility must be treated separately from ordinary WBCS administrative-service eligibility.

Additional conditions may include:

- physical standard;
- medical standard;
- service-specific requirements.

The physical requirement must be linked to the applicable WBCS examination rule rather than inferred from Kolkata Police or West Bengal Police Sub-Inspector recruitment.

---

# 11. Service/Post Separation

The following must remain separate in production data:

- WBCS Executive;
- West Bengal Revenue Service;
- West Bengal Co-operative Service;
- West Bengal Labour Service;
- West Bengal Food and Supplies Service;
- West Bengal Employment Service;
- West Bengal Police Service;
- West Bengal Subordinate Land Revenue Service;
- Joint Block Development Officer;
- Panchayat Development Officer;
- Rehabilitation Officer;
- other services/posts actually specified in the applicable advertisement.

Never treat "WBCS" as one job for pay, duties or family-life scoring.
