---

# 2. Important Interpretation

This status must never be interpreted as:

> Every West Bengal Government post has been discovered.

That claim would not be supportable.

The appropriate statement is:

> The project is building a comprehensive publicly researchable West Bengal government-post universe using department, organisation, designation, recruitment, service-rule, qualification, pay, district, A–Z and job-family discovery methods.

# 20. Current Uncertainty Classes

Any unresolved fact must be labelled:

- NOT_VERIFIED
- REQUIRES_MANUAL_VERIFICATION
- OFFICIAL_HISTORICAL
- CURRENT_ESTIMATE
- SECONDARY_ESTIMATE

# 24. Research Saturation Rule

The project may declare:

**PRACTICAL RESEARCH SATURATION REACHED**

only when repeated research across:

- departments
- directorates
- designations
- recruitment authorities
- service rules
- recruitment rules
- qualification searches
- pay searches
- district portals
- A–Z searches
- job-family searches

stops producing substantial new relevant posts.

Even then, the project must retain:

    "publicly researchable catalogue"

rather than:

    "every government post in existence"

---

# 25. Final Status Statement

The correct current status is:

> West Bengal core recruitment infrastructure has been established, but the research program remains open for designation-level, directorate-level and district-level expansion.

No production dashboard should display "Complete West Bengal Government Jobs" until the final saturation audit is completed.
