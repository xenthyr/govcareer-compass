# GovCareer Compass — West Bengal Department Master Index

## 1. Purpose

This is the master audit index for all West Bengal Government departments identified through the official government directory.

It is a control document.

A department being listed here does not mean:

- the department has a B.A.-eligible post;
- a current vacancy exists;
- direct recruitment exists;
- recruitment occurs through WBPSC;
- recruitment occurs every year.

Those questions must be independently verified.

---

# 2. Audit Status Definitions

### COMPLETE

Department, organisations, cadres/designations, recruitment, rules, qualification and major employment information have been substantially audited.

### SUBSTANTIALLY_COMPLETE

Most important discovery layers have been audited, but limited unresolved areas remain.

### PARTIAL

Initial department/recruitment discovery has been performed but designation/rules or other layers remain incomplete.

### NOT_VERIFIED

Meaningful audit not yet completed.

# 5. Mandatory Department Audit Questions

For every department answer:

### Identity

- What is the official department name?
- What official URL exists?
- What directorates exist?
- What organisations are attached?

### Establishment

- What cadres exist?
- What sanctioned designations are documented?
- What staff categories exist?

### Recruitment

- Who recruits?
- Is recruitment direct?
- Is recruitment by promotion?
- Is recruitment by deputation?
- Is recruitment contractual?

### Qualification

- Class 8?
- Class 10?
- Class 12?
- Graduate?
- Specialist?
- Skill?
- Language?
- Physical?
- Medical?
- Experience?

### Pay

- ROPA level?
- starting basic?
- maximum basic?
- allowances?
- special allowances?

### Career

- duties?
- posting?
- transfer?
- promotion?
- training?
- probation?
- retirement?

### Family

- office/field?
- night duty?
- transfer burden?
- Kolkata stability?
- housing?

### Evidence

- primary source?
- historical source?
- unresolved conflict?
- confidence?

# 6. Department Completion Gate

A department cannot be marked COMPLETE unless its audit record includes:

- department identity
- organisation discovery
- recruitment authority
- cadre/designation discovery
- service/recruitment rules
- current recruitment search
- historical recruitment search
- qualification search
- pay search
- district/field search where applicable
- unresolved questions

# 8. Audit Warning

A department marked PARTIAL must not be interpreted by the website as:

    "no jobs available"

It means:

    "the research universe has not yet been completely audited."

This distinction is mandatory.
