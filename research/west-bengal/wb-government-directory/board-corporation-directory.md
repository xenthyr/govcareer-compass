## Document Status

- Document ID: `research-wb-boards-corporations-directory`
- Scope: State boards, corporations, authorities and government-linked organisations
- Research baseline: `2026-08-31`
- Status: `ACTIVE_DISCOVERY_REGISTER`

---

# 1. Purpose

The Government of West Bengal ecosystem includes organisations that are not ordinary departments.

Examples include:

- boards
- authorities
- development authorities
- corporations
- government companies
- statutory bodies
- municipal corporations
- government-linked enterprises

These must be discovered separately.

---

# 2. Critical Classification Rule

The project must distinguish:

    STATE GOVERNMENT DEPARTMENT
        ≠
    LOCAL GOVERNMENT BODY
        ≠
    STATUTORY AUTHORITY
        ≠
    GOVERNMENT CORPORATION
        ≠
    PSU
        ≠
    GOVERNMENT-CONTROLLED COMPANY
        ≠
    AUTONOMOUS INSTITUTION

A job in one category must not automatically appear as the same type of government career as another category.

---

# 3. Primary Official Discovery Source

Government of West Bengal — Government Directories:

https://wb.gov.in/government-directories.aspx

The official government directory contains a dedicated Corporations/Boards section and currently exposes organisations including development authorities, municipal bodies, infrastructure organisations, utilities, corporations and other government-linked entities. 1

# 9. Critical Career Database Rule

Do not put:

    government corporation employee

into the same career category as:

    WBCS officer

without visibly identifying the difference.

The website should allow users to filter:

    State Government
    Local Government
    Statutory Body
    Corporation
    PSU
    Autonomous Body

separately.

# 11. Production Promotion Rule

An organisation should enter:

    data/states/west-bengal/organisations.json

only after identity and status are sufficiently verified.

Posts should enter:

    data/states/west-bengal/jobs.json

only after:

- post identity
- recruitment route
- qualification
- employment category
- source evidence

have been established.

---

# 12. Official Directory Principle

The official Government of West Bengal directory is a discovery source.

It is not, by itself:

- a recruitment notice;
- a qualification rule;
- a pay rule;
- proof of current vacancy;
- proof of regular government service.

---

# 13. Research Conclusion

Boards, corporations and authorities are an important part of the broader GovCareer Compass universe, but they require strict classification.

The project must preserve the distinction between:

    Civil Service
    Police Service
    Local Government
    Statutory Body
    Corporation
    PSU
    Autonomous Organisation

This distinction is mandatory for accurate career recommendations.
