# 1. Purpose

District-level research is mandatory because important West Bengal government recruitment may occur through:

- district establishments
- District Magistrate/Collectorate offices
- Zilla Parishads
- Panchayat Samitis
- Gram Panchayats
- district welfare offices
- district disaster-management offices
- district social-welfare offices
- district employment offices
- district education structures
- municipal institutions
- district-specific recruitment notices

A state-level recruitment search alone is therefore insufficient.

For EVERY district investigate:

- District Magistrate
- Collectorate
- Additional District Magistrate
- Sub-Divisional Offices
- Block Development Offices
- district establishment sections

## Rural Government

- Zilla Parishad
- Panchayat Samiti
- Gram Panchayat
- Panchayat recruitment
- rural-development offices

## Social Welfare

- Women and Child Development
- Social Welfare
- Backward Classes Welfare
- Tribal Development
- Child Protection

## Disaster

- District Disaster Management
- Civil Defence
- emergency administration

## Labour / Employment

- Labour office
- Employment office
- welfare offices

- district school administration
- education offices
- non-teaching recruitment
- education-related district establishments

- land records
- revenue offices
- registration/stamp structures

- food/supplies
- consumer affairs
- agriculture
- fisheries
- forest
- transport
- municipal administration
- other district offices

# 5. District Recruitment Classification

Every discovered vacancy must be classified as:

### REGULAR_STATE_SERVICE

A regular state-government cadre/post.

### LOCAL_GOVERNMENT

Municipal/Panchayat/local-body employment.

### CONTRACTUAL

Contract appointment.

### SCHEME_PROJECT

Government scheme/project post.

### OUTSOURCED

Outsourced staffing.

### TEMPORARY

Temporary appointment.

### DEPUTATION

Deputation-based appointment.

### UNKNOWN

Insufficient evidence.

# 6. District Recruitment Evidence

For each recruitment notice record:

- district
- department
- organisation
- post

- notification date
- application period
- vacancy
- qualification
- age
- reservation
- recruitment route
- employment status
- pay
- source ID
- current status

---

# 7. District-Specific Eligibility Rule

Do not assume that because a post exists in one district it exists identically in every district.

District recruitment may differ because of:

- local body
- project
- establishment
- scheme
- sanctioned strength
- district-specific rules

Each district record must be independently verified.

# 9. Kolkata Special Rule

The project must never interpret:

    Kolkata Government Office
        =
    Kolkata-only career

A state-wide cadre may have an office in Kolkata while still carrying statewide transfer/posting conditions.

Kolkata stability must therefore be a post-specific analytical field.

---

# 10. District Housing Research

Where relevant investigate:

- government accommodation
- departmental quarters
- block/rural accommodation
- police accommodation
- hostel/barrack
- availability
- allotment
- licence fee
- HRA effect

Do not assume district posting provides housing.

---

# 11. District Family-Life Research

For district-based posts evaluate:

- rural probability
- geographic stability
- commute
- transfer
- night duty
- emergency duty
- family relocation
- parent-care implications

These are analytical assessments and must remain separate from official facts.

---

# 12. District Completion Criteria

A district may be marked:

### COMPLETE

when:

- official identity checked;
- Collectorate checked;
- district recruitment page checked;
- Panchayat/local-government sources checked;
- relevant department offices checked;
- current and historical recruitment checked;
- important unresolved posts recorded.

### SUBSTANTIALLY_COMPLETE

Most major discovery layers checked.

### PARTIAL

Initial discovery only.

### NOT_VERIFIED

Meaningful official discovery not yet completed.

---

# 13. District Research Saturation

District research reaches practical saturation when repeated searches across:

- district administration
- local government
- recruitment
- department offices
- designation families
- qualification terms
- official notices

stop producing substantial new relevant government-service posts.

---

# 14. District Research Principle

District recruitment is an omission-detection mechanism.

It should not be merged into the state-level dataset merely because the same department name appears at state level.
