# GovCareer Compass — West Bengal Research Manifest

## 1. Purpose

This document defines exactly what must be researched for the West Bengal government layer before the research program can be considered practically saturated.

The objective is not merely to collect recruitment advertisements.

The objective is to reconstruct the government-post universe sufficiently to answer:

- What posts exist?
- Which departments own them?
- Which cadres do they belong to?
- How are they recruited?
- What qualification is required?
- Can a B.A. English Honours graduate apply?
- What additional qualification may be required?
- What is the current/historical status?
- What is the applicable pay?
- What are the principal duties?
- Where can the employee be posted?
- What is the transfer profile?
- What are the physical/medical requirements?
- What is the promotion route?
- What housing or benefits may apply?
- How reliable is the evidence?

---

# 2. Master Discovery Layers

Research must use all of the following layers.

## Layer A — Government Department Discovery

Start from the official Government of West Bengal department directory.

For every department identify:

- department name
- official URL
- senior administrative structure
- attached offices
- directorates
- boards
- commissions
- corporations
- statutory authorities
- known recruitment authorities
- official recruitment page
- official rules page
- official publications
- annual reports
- RTI/establishment information where available

---

## Layer B — Organisation Discovery

For every department identify relevant:

- directorates
- commissionerates
- boards
- authorities
- commissions
- secretariats
- attached offices
- field offices
- district offices
- development boards
- statutory bodies

An organisation must not be treated as a department merely because its website appears under a departmental portal.

---

## Layer C — Cadre and Designation Discovery

Search for:

- sanctioned strength
- cadre strength
- establishment schedules
- designation lists
- staffing patterns
- organisational charts
- manpower statements
- service rules
- recruitment rules
- gazettes

The goal is to discover post names before relying on current vacancies.

## Layer D — Recruitment Discovery

- WBPSC
- WBPRB
- Kolkata Police
- West Bengal Police
- department recruitment pages
- directorate recruitment pages
- district recruitment pages

- official notices

- official result pages

# 19. Lower-Qualification Audit

Every Class 8, Class 10 and Class 12 post must receive an overqualification assessment.

Allowed classification:

- HIGHER_QUALIFICATION_EXPLICITLY_ALLOWED
- HIGHER_QUALIFICATION_NOT_ADDRESSED
- HIGHER_QUALIFICATION_RESTRICTED
- EXACT_QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED
- CURRENT_NOTIFICATION_REQUIRED
- UNKNOWN

The system must never automatically assume:

    graduate
       =
    eligible for every lower-level post

# 25. Research Completion Criteria

A department may be marked:

### COMPLETE

when:

- department identity verified
- organisations checked
- recruitment pages checked
- service rules checked
- recruitment rules checked
- designation/cadre information checked where available
- relevant district structures checked
- current/historical recruitment checked
- eligibility checked
- pay checked
- unresolved issues recorded

### SUBSTANTIALLY_COMPLETE

When most material layers have been checked but one or more non-critical evidence layers remain.

### PARTIAL

When only recruitment pages or a limited selection of sources has been checked.

### NOT_VERIFIED

When meaningful research could not yet be completed.

# 26. Saturation Principle

The research program reaches:

**PRACTICAL RESEARCH SATURATION**

only when repeated searches across:

- department names
- directorates
- sanctioned designations
- service rules
- recruitment rules
- recruitment advertisements
- district portals
- designation families
- qualification terms
- pay levels
- A–Z searches

stop generating substantial new B.A.-relevant government-service posts.

Saturation is not absolute completeness.

---

# 28. Manifest Maintenance

Whenever a new research family is discovered:

1. add it to the manifest;
2. create the appropriate research folder;
3. research the family;
4. record evidence;
5. audit eligibility;
6. promote verified data only afterward.

The manifest must evolve with the project rather than becoming frozen.
