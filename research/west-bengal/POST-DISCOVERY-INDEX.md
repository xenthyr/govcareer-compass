# GovCareer Compass — West Bengal Post Discovery Index

## 1. Purpose

This document is the central inventory of discovered government designations/posts before and during eligibility verification.

It is a discovery index, not automatically the final candidate database.

A post may appear here even when:

- it is promotion-only;
- it is deputation-only;
- it is obsolete;
- it is specialist;
- it is currently not recruiting;
- it is not eligible for B.A. English;
- its current qualification is unresolved.

---

# 20. Specialist Exclusion Control

The discovery index may contain specialist posts.

These must not be automatically promoted into the B.A. English candidate database.

Examples:

- engineering posts
- medical posts
- nursing posts
- pharmacy posts
- science-specialist posts
- agriculture-specialist posts
- law posts
- library-specialist posts
- IT specialist posts
- professional accounts posts

The post remains in discovery until a qualification decision is recorded.

# 23. B.A. English Eligibility

Use:

### DIRECT

The B.A. English Honours degree directly satisfies the academic requirement.

### CONDITIONAL

The degree is academically sufficient, but another requirement must be satisfied.

### NOT_ELIGIBLE

Mandatory qualification is missing.

### MANUAL_VERIFICATION

The available evidence does not safely establish eligibility.

---

# 24. Discovery-to-Production Rule

A post may enter:

    data/states/west-bengal/jobs.json

only after:

- identity is verified;
- qualification is verified;
- recruitment route is verified;
- current/historical status is verified;
- sources are linked;
- schema validation succeeds.

---

# 25. Duplicate Control

Similar titles must not automatically be merged.

For example:

    Sub-Inspector
    Sub-Inspector of Police
    Sub-Inspector of Excise
    Sub-Inspector of Correctional Administration

must remain separate unless official evidence establishes that they are actually the same cadre/post.

Likewise:

    Assistant
    Assistant in Department X
    Assistant in Department Y

may be separate cadres even when the names are identical.

---

# 26. Missing-Post Control

When a post is newly discovered:

1. assign a research record;
2. record discovery method;
3. identify source;
4. establish department;
5. establish organisation;
6. establish cadre;
7. verify recruitment;
8. verify qualification;
9. classify B.A. eligibility;
10. send to production only after verification.
